App Dashboard privacy

App Dashboard is a private tool by Hadi Mohammadi for viewing their own app numbers. With the owner's approval it reads AdMob reports (earnings, ad requests, ads shown) and cannot change anything in AdMob.

The totals are stored in a private database on Cloudflare and shown only to the owner and people they choose. They are not sold or used for advertising. No player data is collected.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
